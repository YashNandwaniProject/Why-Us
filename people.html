Why 2iResourcing?

202

2iResourcing, a division of 2iSolutions Inc., delivers expert IT consulting and staffing solutions. With a client-centric focus, we tailor services to meet industry needs. As a leader among staffing agencies in Canada, we bring innovation, reliability, and proven success across diverse sectors—your partner in excellence and smart workforce strategies.

For Employers

Access top-tier IT talent and tailored solutions to meet your business needs with one of the leading employment agencies in Canada, helping you stay ahead with future job opportunities that drive success.

Why 2iresourcing? We get results

Our unique sourcing methodology ensures that we find the right talent for you every single time.

2iresourcing is a different kind of IT Resource Company. It utilizes a Hybrid approach to IT resource placement practice using a mix of IT consultants (available from IT consulting division) and staffing resources (available from IT staffing division).

  1. 01

    Experienced Staff

    With over 19 years of staffing experience, 2iresourcing employs highly professional and caring Recruiting professionals that manage the full staffing cycle and represent clients professionally throughout the process.

  2. 02

    Extensive IT Resource Database

    2iresourcing has an inhouse Database/ATS of more than 250,000 candidates with a wide range of IT and Business skills and experience. This pool of database has helped 2iresourcing in providing the best fit of resources and replacement within a small time frame for public and private sector clients of all sizes.

  3. 03

    Find talent effortlessly

    As endless amounts of resumes line up in your inbox, finding the gems can take more time than your organization can afford. With 2iresourcing’s employment services you get over 19 years of expertise to help you sift through the pile. Effortlessly find the right talent as we help you make the right decisions for your organization.

  4. 04

    Bespoke hiring services

    Our hiring services are tailor-made to your exact specifications. With an intimate and intricate understanding of the job market, we extensively filter candidates to identify the right ones for a role. 2iresourcing picks the best of the lot for the specific talent needs of your organization, insuring your new hire fits like a glove.

  5. 05

    Building Relationships

    We know that aligning the professional qualifications of consultants with the technical requirements of our clients is only one part of what we do. Our real job is building trusted relationships—relationships that work to deliver competitive advantage for our clients and rewarding opportunities for our consultants.

  6. 06

    2iresourcing is not a traditional recruitment agency

    Traditional recruitment agencies utilize only the recruiters (non-technical staff) to short list and qualify the candidates. In today’s complex technology world it is not possible for those who do not know the technology to short list and qualify the candidates. For this reason 2iResourcing use a hybrid team of IT Consultants and recruitment staff to shortlist and qualify the candidates. Our uniqueness lies in our ability to service both candidates and clients, providing a range of services that complement the traditional recruitment agency.
